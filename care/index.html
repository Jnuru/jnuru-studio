QUESTIONS / 04

What people ask about care plans.

What counts as an edit?

Anything small that keeps the site current: swapping photos, updating hours or prices, rewriting a paragraph, adding a team member, fixing a link, or adding a short new section. Plans are measured in time, not number of requests, so a handful of quick changes fit easily.

What if I need more than my plan covers?

Bigger work like a new feature, a full new section of the site, or a redesign gets a quick quote first. Nothing extra gets billed without you saying yes.

Can I cancel a website care plan?

Yes, anytime. If you leave, you get your full site code and help moving it. Your website is never held hostage.

Do unused hours roll over?

No. Each month starts fresh. That keeps the price low and the turnaround fast.
