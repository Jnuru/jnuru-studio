CASE STUDY • STUDIO CLIENT

An author site that sells books, talks, and editing in one scroll

A personal website for a New Testament scholar, author, and theologian, built so publishers, churches, and writers can each find what they came for fast.

In plain terms: one person, three different audiences. The site tells each of them where to go in the first screen.

Client Rubin McClain • scholar, author, theologian
Role Design • Copy structure • Build • Launch
Scope Personal brand site: books, writing, speaking, editorial services, contact
Timeline 2026
Continue →

01 / Brief

Three audiences, one person, no clear front door.

Rubin's work spans academic books, articles for national outlets, speaking for churches and universities, and paid manuscript editing. Each of those audiences needed to see themselves on the site without wading through the others.

  • Two books with different publishers and different readers
  • Speaking, workshops, book studies, and writers' retreats
  • Editorial and publishing support with real pricing
  • Recent writing from outside publications
  • One contact path that still sorts inquiries by type

02 / Approach

Lead with identity, then route by need.

The structure does the sorting. A short tagline and intro set the tone, then three buttons send each visitor to their section. Everything below follows that same order.

Step 1

Bold, confident type

A condensed all-caps display face against a dark ground gives a scholar's site some backbone without feeling stuffy.

Step 2

Paths in the hero

Explore Books, Speaking & Workshops, and Editorial Support sit right under the intro, so nobody has to scroll to find their door.

Step 3

Pricing out loud

Editorial rates are listed plainly, which filters inquiries and saves a back and forth email.

03 / Details

Small decisions that make it easier to use.

Most of the craft is in things visitors never notice, because nothing gets in their way.

  • Contact form with inquiry types: speaking, workshop, book study, retreat, editorial, coaching, so every message arrives pre-sorted
  • External links marked with ↗: readers always know when they're leaving for a publisher or store
  • Skip to content link and responsive menu for keyboard and mobile visitors
  • Recent writing as cards pulling from three outside publications, with a path to the full archive
  • Client-owned accounts: Rubin owns the GitHub and Netlify accounts, with jnuru.studio added as a collaborator

04 / Outcome

A site that works as hard as his CV, and reads a lot better.

01

Clear front door

Publishers, event organizers, and writers each have an obvious path from the first screen.

02

Fewer dead-end emails

Inquiry types and listed rates mean conversations start further along.

03

Owned and editable

Built on his own accounts so it stays his, with room to add a cover, a book, or a talk.
