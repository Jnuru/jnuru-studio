CASE STUDY • STUDIO CLIENT

A kids music brand that feels like a hug, and still works for parents

A website and link-in-bio hub for Joyful Songhouse, a children's music channel making catchy sing and dance songs starring Squiddo the squid.

In plain terms: kids see the squid, parents find the songs. Every tap from Instagram lands somewhere useful.

Client Joyful Songhouse • kids music & video channel
Role Web design • Build • Link-in-bio page • Launch
Scope Homepage, Listen & Watch links page, privacy policy
Tools Hand-coded HTML, CSS & JS
Timeline 2026
Continue →

01 / Brief

Kids are the audience. Parents are the user.

A children's brand has to delight a three year old and earn the trust of the adult holding the phone. The site needed to feel playful without being loud, and get parents to the songs in one or two taps.

  • Introduce the brand and its mascot, Squiddo
  • Feature the first single, Squid Squirm, with streaming links
  • Give parents something to do with the music, not just play it
  • Turn Instagram's single bio link into a real hub
  • Stay calm: nothing overstimulating for little ones

02 / Approach

Soft, friendly, and easy to tap.

The look comes from the brand's own crayon-style illustrations. The structure stays simple so the characters do the talking.

Step 1

A palette from the art

Cream background with the pink, yellow, and sky blue pulled straight from Squiddo and friends.

Step 2

Rounded type

Fredoka for headlines and Nunito for body text, friendly at a glance and easy for parents to read.

Step 3

Big, obvious buttons

Every action is a large rounded pill, sized for thumbs and for quick decisions.

03 / Details

Built for families, down to the small stuff.

A kids brand earns trust in the details parents notice later.

  • Custom link-in-bio page instead of a third-party tool, so every Instagram tap stays on brand and on their own domain
  • Pause controls and reduced motion support: animations stop for anyone whose device asks for less motion, and there's a visible pause button
  • A note for parents with simple ways to turn a song into a family dance party
  • Privacy policy and a clear supervise-play reminder in the footer
  • Social links up front: YouTube in the nav, plus Apple Music, Spotify, and Amazon Music on the featured song

04 / Outcome

A brand home that's as joyful as the songs.

01

One hub for everything

Streaming, video, and the website all live under joyfulsonghouse.com.

02

Parent-friendly by design

Calm motion, clear actions, and real guidance for playing together.

03

Ready to grow

New songs and videos slot into the same pattern as the catalog grows.
